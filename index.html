04 / A Traceable Process

Resolve the questions.
Coordinate the steel.

01

Review

Examine supplied engineering drawings, specifications and scope. Identify missing dimensions, levels and interfaces before modelling starts.

02

Coordinate

Build the 3D Tekla model against agreed information, coordinate member geometry, and raise focused RFIs to confirm unclear requirements.

03

Detail & Check

Detail connections with tool clearance and welding access in mind. Perform internal checks across marks, dimensions, orientation and clashes.

04

Issue & Revise

Deliver agreed fabrication drawings, erection plans, MTO reports and supporting files. Maintain disciplined revision records as changes occur.
